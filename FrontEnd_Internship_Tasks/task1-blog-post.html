Why Semantic HTML Still Matters

By

N. Somasekhar Naidu
· Published on 12 October 2026

Introduction

Every page can be built from nothing but <div> elements, and it will look fine. But browsers, search engines and screen readers cannot tell what those boxes mean. Semantic HTML gives each part of the page a name.

Main Content: What You Gain

  • Accessibility: screen readers can jump straight to <main> or <nav>.
  • SEO: search engines understand headings, articles and dates.
  • Maintainability: teammates read <article> faster than <div class="box3">.
  • Free behaviour: native elements such as <button> and <details> handle keyboard and focus for you.

Conclusion

Choose the element that describes the content, then style it. Semantic markup costs nothing extra and makes every later step (CSS, JavaScript, testing) easier.
